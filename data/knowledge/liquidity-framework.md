# Global Liquidity Framework — Complete Reference

## What Is Global Liquidity?

Global Liquidity (~$185T as of mid-2025) = aggregated pool of international cash savings and credits flowing through the **financial system**. NOT traditional M2/M3 (which measures real-economy deposits). It measures **balance sheet capacity** of credit providers.

### The Liquidity Pyramid
- Private Sector Liquidity: $175T total (investable)
- Shadow Monetary Base: $120-165T
  - Central Bank $ (least volatile)
  - Collateral (rehypothecated 3-4x)
  - CrossBorder: FX swaps, Eurodollar (most volatile)

Leverage ratio: ~1.5x (normal 1.4-1.6x, crisis <1.2x, bubble >1.8x).

## The Master Formula: P = (P/L) × L
- P = asset price, L = Global Liquidity, P/L = valuation multiple on liquidity
- P explains ~80% of P/E movements
- Liquidity is more important than interest rates in determining asset prices

## Fed Liquidity (Key US Metric)

Fed Liquidity = Reserve Bank Credit − RRP − TGA − Currency in Circulation

This is what matters — NOT the Fed balance sheet size.

- Fed Liquidity leads S&P 500 by ~6 months (26 weeks)
- Fed Liquidity leads Bitcoin by ~6 weeks (Granger causality) — Fed easing is the strongest single driver of BTC (direct USD liquidity injection → risk appetite → BTC as highest-beta liquidity expression)
- PBoC Liquidity leads Gold/Commodities by ~13 weeks (primary) — PBoC easing → gold & commodities, NOT BTC as primary play. PBoC → BTC transmission is indirect/weaker

### Components
- Reserve Bank Credit (~$6.5T): OMO, discount window, SRF, RMP, operating losses
- RRP: Was $2.7T peak (2023), now near zero. Rundown added ~$2.4T. No more tailwind
- TGA: Target ~$850B. Seasonal (Jan high +$200B, Aug-Dec low). Cumulative swings ~$400B
- RMP (Reserve Management Purchases): $40B/month — "Not QE, QE"

## Four Factors Affecting Global Liquidity (Howell Empirical)

- World economic growth (+1 std dev) → -1 std dev GLI (drains liquidity to real economy)
- DXY (+10%) → -7-8 GLI index points → -15-20% world equities
- Oil prices (-10%) → +~5 GLI index points
- MOVE Index (-10 points) → +$2.25T Global Liquidity

Together explain ~50% of monthly GLI variation.

### DXY Dynamics
- Strong USD = deflationary for global liquidity BUT US equities partially shielded
- Weak USD = foreign markets outperform, commodities rally, EM benefits
- Each 10% DXY rise → 15-20% fall in world equities (ex-US hit hardest)

### MOVE Index
- Sub-100: Expanding collateral multiplier → more liquidity
- Above 100: Contracting multiplier → squeeze
- Bond volatility is a superior systemic risk indicator to VIX

### Debt/Liquidity Ratio
- Equilibrium ~2.0-2.5x. Mean-reverting
- $350T world debt, ~5yr avg maturity → $70T/year must be refinanced
- 75% of primary transactions = debt rollovers, not new capital
- Debt maturity wall 2025-2029: $10T+ of COVID-era termed-out debt coming due

## Types of QE and Market Impact

- Fed QE (long-duration bonds): Reduces duration supply → portfolio rebalancing to risk assets. Benefits Wall Street / financial assets.
- Treasury QE (T-Bills): Banks monetize bills → balance sheet expansion. Benefits Main Street / real economy.
- Not QE, QE (RMP, SRF, losses): Plumbing stabilization. Benefits money markets.

2026 outlook: Stimulus dominated by Treasury QE → benefits real economy > financial markets → range-bound stock market rather than strong uptrend.

- SOFR > EFFR sustained = collateral shortage (tightening)

## Operational Phase → Sector & Asset Allocation Matrix

This matrix is a **probabilistic guide**, not a rigid law. Combine with liquidity regime, inflation, yield curve, price action, breadth, and credit spreads.

Rating legend: **verde** = favorable, **arancione** = neutral/mixed, **rosso** = unfavorable.

### REBOUND (exit correction, liquidity improving, cautious sentiment)

The market is recovering from fear. Liquidity improves or stops deteriorating. Sentiment is still cautious, prices start from depressed levels, investors re-enter risk.

| Category | Rating | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Beta / Risk-On | arancione | Emerging, but don't overdo — sentiment still fragile |
| Equities | verde | Core allocation — market prices in future improvement |
| Credits | verde | Spreads tightening, risk perception improving |
| Commodities | rosso | Not yet the dominant theme |
| Bond Duration | rosso | Recession fear fading → less demand for duration |
| Cyclicals | verde | Most sensitive to macro improvement |
| Technology | verde | Leads rerating, quality-growth rebounds first |
| Financials | arancione | Partial benefit from curve expectations |
| Energy | rosso | Not dominant yet |
| Defensives | rosso | Underperform as market seeks risk |

**Best:** Quality-growth stocks that corrected too much, solid-balance-sheet cyclicals, IG credit.
**Worst:** Long bonds, defensives, commodities/energy.

### CALM (stable growth, low volatility, favorable liquidity)

Most orderly phase. Growth stable, volatility contained, inflation not destabilizing, liquidity supportive. Broadest participation in equity rally.

| Category | Rating | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Beta / Risk-On | verde | Full risk-on environment |
| Equities | verde | Broadest positive participation |
| Credits | arancione | OK but less edge than Rebound |
| Commodities | verde | Good demand supports real assets |
| Bond Duration | rosso | Stable growth → low demand for defensive duration |
| Cyclicals | verde | Pro-growth, earnings visibility |
| Technology | verde | Leadership continues |
| Financials | verde | Nominal growth + reasonable yields → banks do well |
| Energy | verde | Good demand |
| Defensives | rosso | Investors seek return, not protection |

**Best:** Broad equity, tech, cyclicals, financials, energy, commodities — nearly everything except defensives.
**Worst:** Duration, defensives.

### SPECULATION (euphoria, crowded positioning, elevated multiples, overheating risk)

The most delicate phase. Market is NOT simply "strong" — it is crowded, extended, with high positioning, elevated multiples, dominant narratives, and risk of overheating. The risk/reward ratio deteriorates significantly.

| Category | Rating | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Beta / Risk-On | arancione | Upside/risk ratio worsening |
| Equities | arancione | Be much more selective, don't chase |
| Credits | rosso | Most vulnerable — very sensitive to risk increase |
| Commodities | verde | Hold if theme is growth/inflation/scarcity |
| Bond Duration | arancione | Can begin to serve as stabilizer |
| Cyclicals | rosso | Too crowded, too extended |
| Technology | rosso | Crowded, blow-off risk |
| Financials | arancione | Mixed — can hold but no longer a leader |
| Energy | verde | Benefits from nominal growth/inflation/scarcity |
| Defensives | verde | Returning as stabilizers as risk under surface builds |

**Best:** Commodities, energy, defensives.
**Weak:** Tech, cyclicals, credits — upside/risk ratio deteriorates, too crowded to chase.
**Key rule:** Reduce aggressiveness, don't chase extended breakouts, rotate toward real assets/inflation hedges, start adding defensive stabilizers. Selection matters enormously.

### TURBULENCE (growth decelerating, vol high, spreads widening, risk-off)

True stress regime. Growth decelerating, volatility elevated, credit spreads widening, market seeks safety.

| Category | Rating | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Beta / Risk-On | rosso | Maximum pain |
| Equities | rosso | Broad drawdown |
| Credits | arancione | Not great but quality credit can hold |
| Commodities | rosso | Demand destruction |
| Bond Duration | verde | Flight to quality, yields drop |
| Cyclicals | rosso | Most macro-sensitive, worst drawdown |
| Technology | rosso | Duration + growth sensitivity = pain |
| Financials | rosso | Credit stress, NIM pressure |
| Energy | rosso | Demand collapse |
| Defensives | verde | Stable earnings, low cycle sensitivity |

**Best:** Bond duration, defensives (stable earnings, quality, protection).
**Worst:** Everything else — beta, equities, cyclicals, tech, financials, energy, commodities.
Quality credit (arancione) is acceptable but not ideal.

## Phase Transition Detection Rules

### First Signal
- GLI first derivative sign change (momentum direction turning)
- This is the early warning — not yet confirmed

### Confirmation
- 2 consecutive weeks of same-direction GLI change after the sign flip
- Cross-validate with: breadth divergence, credit spreads movement, yield curve shape change, MOVE index

### Transition Map
| From | To | Trigger |
|------|-------|---------|
| Turbulence | Rebound | GLI first derivative turns positive from deep negative |
| Rebound | Calm | GLI remains positive but decelerates (growth normalizing) |
| Calm | Speculation | GLI re-accelerates, asset leverage rises >1.6× |
| Speculation | Turbulence | GLI turns negative, leverage unwinding begins |

### Lead Times (Critical for Positioning)
- Fed liquidity change → S&P 500 response: ~6 months (26 weeks)
- Fed easing → BTC response: ~6 weeks (strongest single BTC driver)
- PBoC easing → Gold/Commodities response: ~13 weeks (primary)
- PBoC easing → BTC: indirect/weaker (NOT the primary BTC driver)
